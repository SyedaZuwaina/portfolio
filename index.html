The hook

An explainable AI meal decision engine that translates Western nutrition and Traditional Chinese Medicine (TCM) energetics into safe, pantry-staple recipes during acute physical discomfort.

The problem triad

The frustrating moment

Bloated, sluggish, or chilled, staring at the kitchen with no mental energy to read conflicting wellness blogs or count macros.

47.8% default to “whatever is easiest” (11/23)

Why it’s hard

Unwell users run on low-bandwidth “System 1” thinking. LLMs can also ignore thermal contraindications, like suggesting warming ginger to an overheated user. In my testing, one did.

34.8% are skeptical of AI meal advice (8/23)

Who it’s for

Everyday adults with recurring mild discomfort who want a safe, explained meal in under 10 seconds, using groceries they already own.

65.2% have symptoms a few times a week or more (15/23)

Competitor landscape and the gap
screenshot slot

MyFitnessPal / macro trackers

Great at long-term calorie logging.

The gap: adds cognitive stress when you feel unwell. 91.3% say macro counting is irrelevant or stressful then (21/23).

screenshot slot

Google Search / wellness blogs

Comprehensive information.

The gap: high reading friction and contradictory advice, with no real-time body context.

screenshot slot

Generic ChatGPT prompts

Fast conversational answers.

The gap: black-box “trust me” output with no deterministic guardrails or ingredient boundaries by default.

The core product insight

Decouple safety from AI generation. Use deterministic Python rules to block red flags and contraindications before the LLM ever sees the prompt, then use AI strictly for constrained synthesis and plain-English explanation.

The solution and interaction flow
Step 1

1-tap somatic input

Tap a symptom plus thermal state (Chilly / Neutral / Warm) in under 10 seconds.

Step 2

Deterministic shield

Python regex intercepts 27 emergency red flags (0 ms LLM compute), then hard-filters thermal contraindications from a 12-staple pantry RAG (knowledge_base.json).

Step 3

Dual-framework XAI card

Groq (openai/gpt-oss-20b) writes a 10-minute recipe with side-by-side Western + TCM rationales. If it fails, a zero-LLM fallback (build_local_fallback_recipe()) serves a safe recipe.

Known limits, stated up front: red-flag matching is keyword-based (paraphrase recall is a pilot task), dosage caps are not yet verified in code after generation, and TCR / CSAT are target KPIs awaiting a pilot cohort.

ThyroHour

Try Live App Read Notion Case Study
The hook

A zero-input morning fasting timer and clinical adherence ledger built for hypothyroidism patients taking Levothyroxine.

The problem triad

The frustrating moment

Heavy morning brain fog. You swallow your thyroid pill and can’t remember whether it was 6:15 or 6:35, so you break the 60-minute fast early with coffee, or skip tracking altogether.

Why it’s hard

Fog kills multi-step onboarding. Meanwhile, eating too soon can reduce Levothyroxine absorption, and a missed window is one possible cause of stalled lab results that nobody is tracking.

Who it’s for

People with hypothyroidism, built from my own lived N = 1 experience, who need a clear line between pill drop, coffee, and the 4-hour calcium/iron gap.

Honest status: no formal user research yet. Interviews are the next step.

Competitor landscape and the gap
screenshot slot

Apple / Android clock alarms

Reliable countdown.

The gap: no clinical context, no 4-hour supplement reminder, and zero adherence history for doctor visits.

screenshot slot

Medisafe / pill reminder apps

Strong multi-medication scheduling.

The gap: built for “Did you take it?”, not “How long until I can eat?”, and heavier than a one-tap launcher.

screenshot slot

Paper / notes-app logs

Flexible journaling.

The gap: too much friction when half asleep, so tracking is abandoned before the endocrinology appointment.

The core product insight

Remove every decision when cognitive bandwidth is lowest. One giant tap starts the isolation window; a distinct 3-state color shift (Amber → Red → Green) and a 00:00 audio chime remove clock-watching anxiety.

The solution and state diagram
State 1 · IDLE · Amber

One tap, no login

A zero-login “Pill Dropped” launcher. The tap also unlocks browser audio for later.

State 2 · COUNTING · Red

Timestamp-synced countdown

A 60-minute timer that survives refreshes, with warning cards: no caffeine or food (1h), no calcium/iron (4h).

State 3 · COMPLETED · Green

Chime, then a quick log

A 00:00 Web Audio API chime, a 1-tap self-reported adherence toggle, and a brain-fog rating logged locally to /logs for doctor reviews.

Known limits: adherence is self-reported, alerts work only while the tab is open, and cancelled fasts aren’t logged yet. I found and fixed an always-100% adherence bug in my own audit.
